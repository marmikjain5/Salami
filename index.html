Why this is suspicious 4 FINDINGS
E01 URGENCY MANIPULATION
HIGH
Creates pressure through an artificial deadline. WHAT WE SAW "Immediately" and "or your loan will expire" set a countdown without giving date or reference. WHY IT MATTERS Pressure shortens the time you have to think or check. Genuine lenders state timelines in writing.
E02 FINANCIAL IMPERSONATION
HIGH
Claims loan approval without verified context. WHAT WE SAW An approval and an amount (₹50,000) appear with no lender name, application number, or earlier request. WHY IT MATTERS Real approvals follow an application you made. Unsolicited approval is the hook for fee demands.
